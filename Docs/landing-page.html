Questions

Do I need internet at the park?

No. Your slip is saved on your phone and opens offline. The worker's scanner confirms it.

Must I pay online?

No. Reserve your space and pay cash when you arrive. Online payment is optional.

What if I miss the bus?

There's a 15-minute grace period after departure. After that your space may be given to someone else.

How do I know my driver?

You see the driver's photo, name, phone, vehicle and plate number before you board — only verified drivers operate.

What if the fare changes after I book?

It can't. Your fare is locked at the moment you book.

How do refunds work?

Cancel more than 6 hours before: full refund (minus small charge). 1–6 hours: half. Under 1 hour: no refund.
